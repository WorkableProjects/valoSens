## Constants to research
1. **Signal-dependent endpoint noise `k`**: endpoint SD as a fraction of movement amplitude for fast aimed hand/mouse movements. Sources: Harris & Wolpert 1998; Schmidt et al. 1979 (impulse variability, We = a + b·A/MT); mouse-pointing endpoint spreads (e.g. Wobbrock et al. on effective width; Zhai et al.).
2. **Joint-specific peak speed and throughput**: finger, wrist, forearm/elbow, whole arm. Sources: Langolf, Chaffin & Foulke 1976; Balakrishnan & MacKenzie 1997; any later work on mouse movement by limb.
3. **Comfortable wrist and forearm excursion on a desk**: radial/ulnar deviation ranges (Ryu et al. 1991 is already used), and mouse-based studies of how far the wrist moves before the forearm takes over.
4. **Control-display gain optimum**: studies varying CD gain for mouse pointing (Casiez, Vogel & Balakrishnan 2008 "The impact of control-display gain on user performance in pointing tasks"; Jellinek & Card 1990; Accot & Zhai 2001 "Scale effects in steering"; Nancel et al.; any FPS-specific ones, including NVIDIA's Boudaoud et al. 2022/2023). Include the shape of the performance curve and where it bottoms out, in gain units and mouse-travel-per-screen terms.
5. **Clutching (mouse lift) time**: per-lift duration (Casiez 2008 gives clutch share; look for per-clutch times, e.g. Casiez & Vogel 2008 "The effect of spring and friction..."; Nancel et al. on clutching).
6. **Submovement duration and count** for corrective phases (Meyer et al. 1988; Elliott et al. 2001/2010 two-component model; Fradet et al. 2008 on submovement parsing).
7. **Pursuit tracking error** with a mouse: position error vs target speed (e.g. Jagacinski & Flach; Huang et al. "moving target selection"; any FPS tracking studies).
8. **Reaction time and visual feedback delay** in aimed movements (~150–250 ms; cite).
9. **Effect of practice/expertise on endpoint variability** (gamers vs non-gamers studies, esports aiming research).
