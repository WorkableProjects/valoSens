# 2026 VCT Pro Player Settings — Expanded Research

**Research date:** 2026-10-31  
**Scope:** Publicly listed settings for players appearing on or associated with VCT-level teams in 2026. This is a best-effort, source-limited dataset—not a claim of every player or every roster change.

## Important data notes

- **Settings source:** The mouse-setting values below are transcribed from [ProSettings.net's VALORANT settings list](https://prosettings.net/lists/valorant/). The site reports mouse polling rate (`Hz`), DPI, sensitivity, and eDPI.
- **VCT roster check:** Use [VLR.gg's 2026 Americas roster thread](https://www.vlr.gg/432155/all-rosters-for-the-season-americas), plus its linked EMEA, Pacific, and China threads, to verify season membership. Settings lists and roster pages can reflect different update dates.
- **Role:** `N/A` means a stable primary role was not independently verified for this row. Roles can change by map.
- **HS%:** `N/A` means a directly comparable VLR.gg headshot percentage was not verified. HS% can vary by event and date range, so it should not be guessed or mixed across different windows.
- **eDPI:** eDPI = DPI × sensitivity. Values below match the source's listed eDPI, with minor decimal rounding where relevant.
- **Polling rate:** `N/A` means the source did not list it for that player.
- Settings are snapshots and may change during the season. These rows are the subset I could verify in the available source results; they are not exhaustive.
