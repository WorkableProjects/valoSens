Valorant Sensitivity Finder

Answer about a dozen questions on your current sensitivity, your mousepad, your role and (optionally) your stats. Instead of adding fixed bonuses for each answer, a cost model estimates the cm/360 that best balances precision, turning speed and pad space for you. VCT 2026 pro settings are shown for comparison only.

Everything runs in your browser and nothing you enter leaves this page. Treat the result as a well-reasoned starting point, then confirm it with the test plan.
